9 — CORE TEACHING  
Paste into: Text (concept)

<h2><strong>Teaching: Treaty of Versailles</strong></h2>  
<p>👉 Let’s break down what the Treaty of Versailles was and what it did, step by step.</p>  

<h3><strong>Key idea:</strong></h3>  
<p>👉 The Treaty was designed to punish Germany and prevent future war but ended up creating tension and problems instead.</p>  

<h3><strong>What happens:</strong></h3>  
<ul>  
<li>The Treaty was written by the winning powers: mainly Britain, France, and the USA at the Paris Peace Conference.</li>  
<li>Germany was forced to accept full blame (the War Guilt Clause), pay huge reparations, reduce its military, and give up territories.</li>  
</ul>  

<h3><strong>Cause → process → effect:</strong></h3>  
<ul>  
<li>Because Germany lost the war and was seen as responsible, the Allies wanted to punish it severely.</li>  
<li>This led to Germany’s economy suffering due to reparations and losing key industrial areas.</li>  
<li>As a result, many Germans felt humiliated, which created political instability and feelings of revenge.</li>  
</ul>  

<h3><strong>Think like an examiner:</strong></h3>  
<p>👉 To answer “Explain why the Treaty caused resentment,” say: “Because Germany was forced to accept blame and pay reparations, its economy weakened and its people felt unfairly treated, which caused deep anger.”</p>

12 — COMMON MISTAKE  
Paste into: Common mistake

<p>👉 A common mistake is thinking the Treaty of Versailles was fair and accepted by all Germans.</p>  
<p>In fact, many Germans strongly disagreed and called it a “Diktat” (dictated peace).</p>  
<p>This matters because understanding German resentment helps explain the rise of extremist parties like the Nazis.</p>

<p><strong>Q3 Answer:</strong> The Treaty caused resentment because Germany was forced to admit full blame, which humiliated the country, and pay huge reparations that damaged its economy, leading to hardship and anger.</p>  

<p><strong>Q4 Answer:</strong> While the Treaty of Versailles was a major cause of WWII because its harsh terms caused German resentment and economic problems, other causes included the Great Depression and the rise of militarism and fascism in Europe. <h2><strong>Summary</strong></h2>  

<ul>  
<li>The Treaty of Versailles was signed in 1919 to end WWI and punish Germany.</li>  
<li>It made Germany accept blame, pay reparations, lose land, and reduce its army.</li>  
<li>These terms caused deep resentment and economic problems in Germany.</li>  
<li>The Treaty affected international relations by sowing bitterness and instability.</li>  
<li>This Treaty is widely seen as a key factor leading to the outbreak of WWII.</li>  
</ul>

24 — KEY WORDS  
Paste into: Key words

<strong>Treaty of Versailles</strong> – The 1919 peace agreement that ended WWI and punished Germany.  
<strong>Reparations</strong> – Money Germany had to pay the Allies for war damage.  
<strong>War Guilt Clause</strong> – Clause blaming Germany for starting WWI.  
<strong>Disarmament</strong> – Reduction of Germany’s military forces after WWI.  
<strong>Territorial Losses</strong> – Areas taken away from Germany after the war.  
<strong>League of Nations</strong> – International organisation created to maintain peace after WWI.  
<strong>Resentment</strong> – Anger and bitterness, especially in Germany over the Treaty.  
<strong>Armistice</strong> – The ceasefire agreement that ended fighting in WWI in 1918.  
<strong>Consequences</strong> – Outcomes or effects of an event, especially long-term ones.  
<strong>Punishment</strong> – Penalties imposed on Germany by the Treaty.
