### Core Learning

**Before:**
```
The lens focuses light and the eye zooms in on near objects.
```

**After:**
```
Light is refracted by the cornea and lens; ciliary muscles change lens shape during accommodation therefore rays converge on the retina.
```

### Common Mistake

**Before:**
```
Students say the retina sees the image.
```

**After:**
```
Students often write: The eye zooms in.
Correct: Ciliary muscles contract leading to a thicker lens and increased refraction.
```

### Worked Example

**Before:**
```
Step 1: Light in. Step 2: Lens bends. Step 3: Retina sees. Step 4: Done.
```

**After:**
```
Step 1 Observation: Light enters through the cornea.
Step 2 Data: The lens refracts rays more strongly during accommodation.
Step 3 Explanation: Photoreceptors in the retina detect the focused image because impulses are sent to the brain.
Step 4 Conclusion: Therefore the eye maintains a clear image on the retina.
```
