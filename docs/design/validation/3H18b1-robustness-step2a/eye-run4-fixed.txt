9— CORE TEACHING
Paste into: Text (concept)
<p>Let's build this step by step.</p>
<h2><strong>Teaching: The eye</strong></h2>
<p>👉 The key idea to focus on is how the eye works as a complete system to detect light and produce clear images.</p>
<h3><strong>What happens:</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Light enters the eye through the transparent cornea first.</li>
<li>The cornea refracts, or bends, the light rays towards the lens; this initial bending is crucial for focusing.</li>
<li>The iris adjusts the size of the pupil to control how much light gets in, protecting the eye in bright light and allowing more light in dim conditions.</li>
<li>The lens lies behind the pupil. It fine-tunes the focus by changing shape through a process called accommodation.</li>
<li>The change in lens shape is controlled by the ciliary muscles and suspensory ligaments working together.</li>
<li>Once focused, the light converges on the retina, a thin layer of light-sensitive cells at the back.</li>
<li>Photoreceptors in the retina detect the light and send electrical signals through the optic nerve to the brain.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Structure → function / Process → effect:</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>The cornea’s curved, transparent structure bends light to help form a focused image.</li>
<li>The iris’s pigmented muscles contract or relax to adjust pupil size, regulating light intensity entering the eye.</li>
<li>Ciliary muscles contract → suspensory ligaments slacken → lens thickens → light refracts more → image of near object forms on the retina clearly.</li>
<li>Ciliary muscles relax → suspensory ligaments tighten → lens flattens → less refraction → distant object image forms on retina.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Think like an examiner:</strong></h3>
<p>👉 In your answers, be sure to use phrases like “light is refracted by the cornea and lens” and explain "ciliary muscles change lens shape for accommodation" to get full marks.</p>
<p>👉 Avoid vague statements like "the eye zooms in" or "the retina sees the image" without explaining how focusing or photoreceptors work.</p>

25— KEY WORDS
Paste into: Key words
<p><strong>👉 Keywords</strong></p>
<strong>Cornea</strong> – The clear, curved outer layer that bends light entering the eye.
<strong>Iris</strong> – The coloured part of the eye that controls pupil size.
<strong>Pupil</strong> – The hole in the iris that lets light into the eye.
<strong>Lens</strong> – The transparent structure that changes shape to focus light on the retina.
<strong>Retina</strong> – The layer of cells at the back of the eye that detects light.
<strong>Ciliary muscles</strong> – Muscles that change tension on ligaments to alter lens shape.
<strong>Suspensory ligaments</strong> – Fibres that connect ciliary muscles to the lens and adjust its shape.
<strong>Accommodation</strong> – The process of changing the lens shape to focus on near or distant objects.
<strong>Refraction</strong> – The bending of light as it passes from one substance to another.
<strong>Photoreceptor</strong> – Light-sensitive cells in the retina that detect images and convert them to electrical signals.
