8— KEY WORDS
Paste into: Key words
<p><strong>👉 Keywords</strong></p>
<strong>Receptor</strong> – A specialised cell or organ that detects a specific stimulus.
<strong>Stimulus</strong> – A change in the environment detected by the body.
<strong>Response</strong> – The action or change produced by an effector after a stimulus.
<strong>Sensory neurone</strong> – A neurone that carries impulses from receptors to the CNS.
<strong>Relay neurone</strong> – A neurone in the CNS that connects sensory and motor neurones.
<strong>Motor neurone</strong> – A neurone that carries impulses from the CNS to effectors.
<strong>Synapse</strong> – The gap between two neurones where impulses are transmitted chemically.
<strong>CNS</strong> – Central Nervous System — the brain and spinal cord.
<strong>PNS</strong> – Peripheral Nervous System — nerves outside the brain and spinal cord.
<strong>Effector</strong> – A muscle or gland that produces a response.
