<h2><strong>Core model</strong></h2>
<p>👉 <strong>The basic homeostasis pathway:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Receptors</strong> detect a stimulus (change in internal/external environment).</li>
<li>Information goes to a <strong>coordination centre</strong> (brain/spinal cord/pancreas).</li>
<li><strong>Effectors</strong> (muscles/glands) act to restore conditions to the <strong>optimum</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<ol>
  <li>Change happens (e.g. temperature rises)</li>
  <li>Receptors sense the change</li>
  <li>Coordination centre processes the signal</li>
  <li>Effector responds (e.g. sweats or releases hormone)</li>
  <li>Internal condition returns to optimum</li>
</ol>

Core Model

<p>Let's build this step by step.</p>
<h2><strong>Core model</strong></h2>
<p>👉 <strong>Stimulus → Receptor → Sensory neurone → CNS (brain or spinal cord) → Motor neurone → Effector → Response</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>A change (stimulus) is detected by a receptor (e.g. in the skin or eye).</li>
<li>The receptor sends an electrical impulse along a sensory neurone to the central nervous system (CNS).</li>
<li>The impulse is processed in the CNS (sometimes via a relay neurone).</li>
<li>An impulse travels along a motor neurone to an effector (muscle or gland).</li>
<li>The effector creates a response (muscle contracts or gland secretes).</li>
<li>This process is extremely rapid—sometimes forming a reflex to protect the body.</li>
</ol>

Core Model

<p>Let's build this step by step.</p>
<h2><strong>Core model</strong></h2>
<p>👉 <strong>Cornea → Lens → Retina</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Light enters the eye through the cornea.</li>
<li>Cornea bends (refracts) most of the incoming light rays.</li>
<li>Iris controls the amount of light that passes through the pupil.</li>
<li>Lens changes shape (accommodation) to focus the image by exactly the right amount.</li>
<li>Focused light falls onto the retina at the back of the eye.</li>
<li>Special cells in the retina detect light and send electrical impulses to the brain for interpretation.</li>
</ol>

<h2><strong>Key examples</strong></h2>
