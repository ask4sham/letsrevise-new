9 — CORE TEACHING  
Paste into: Text (concept)

<h2><strong>Teaching: Historians' interpretations of the Cold War</strong></h2>

<p>👉 The key idea is that historians don't all agree on why the Cold War started or who was to blame.</p>

<h3><strong>What happens:</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Some historians, called <em>orthodox</em>, argue that the USSR’s expansionism caused the Cold War.</li>
<li>Others, known as <em>revisionists</em>, claim American economic and political policies provoked the USSR.</li>
<li><em>Post-revisionists</em> think that fault lies with misunderstandings and mistakes on both sides.</li>
</ul>

<h3><strong>Cause → event → effect:</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Cause: Stalin's aggressive postwar policies → Event: Cold War tensions rise → Effect: Long-lasting rivalry and proxy wars.</li>
<li>Cause: US containment and fear of communism → Event: Truman Doctrine and Marshall Plan → Effect: Deepening East-West divide.</li>
</ul>

<h3><strong>Think like an examiner:</strong></h3>
<p>👉 When you explain causes or consequences, name the interpretation, explain the historian’s argument, and link it to the event or its impact clearly.</p>

16 — EXAM TIP  
Paste into: Exam tip (concept)

<h2><strong>🌍 Why this matters</strong></h2>  
<p>👉 Knowing different interpretations shows your examiner you can analyse history critically, not just memorise facts.</p>  

<h2><strong>🎯 Premium Exam Tip</strong></h2>  
<p>👉 When writing exam answers, always include the historian’s view, explain the reason they give, and link it clearly to the Cold War event or consequence.</p>  

<h2><strong>🎯 Answer Quality Modelling</strong></h2>  
<p>👉 This is how to move from a basic answer to a full-mark explanation.</p>  

<h3><strong>Weak answer:</strong></h3>  
<p>The Cold War started because the USSR was bad.</p>  

<h3><strong>Better answer:</strong></h3>  
<p>The orthodox view says the Cold War started because the USSR wanted to expand its control in Europe.</p>  

<h3><strong>Full-mark answer:</strong></h3>  
<p>The orthodox interpretation argues that the Cold War began due to aggressive Soviet expansion in eastern Europe after 1945, which led the USA to respond with containment policies like the Truman Doctrine, increasing tension between the superpowers.</p>  

<h3><strong>Why the full-mark answer is stronger:</strong></h3>  
<ul>  
<li>It uses precise terminology (“orthodox interpretation,” “containment policies”).</li>  
<li>It explains cause → effect clearly (Soviet expansion → US response → Cold War tension).</li>  
<li>It links interpretation to specific events and policies.</li>  
</ul>

24 — KEY WORDS  
Paste into: Key words

<strong>Interpretation</strong> – A historian’s explanation about why something happened.  
<strong>Orthodox</strong> – The view that the USSR was mainly to blame for the Cold War.  
<strong>Revisionist</strong> – The view that the USA was mainly to blame for the Cold War.  
<strong>Post-revisionist</strong> – The view that both the USA and USSR share blame.  
<strong>Cold War</strong> – The period of tension between the USA and USSR after WWII.  
<strong>Containment</strong> – US policy to stop the spread of communism.  
<strong>Truman Doctrine</strong> – US policy offering help to countries resisting communism.  
<strong>Marshall Plan</strong> – US financial aid to rebuild Western Europe after WWII.  
<strong>Historiography</strong> – The study of how history is written and interpreted.  
<strong>Cause and consequence</strong> – Explaining why something happened and what resulted from it.
